YOUR STUDY PARTNER

Lumi, your LearnIT study buddy

Upload your class material and Lumi will create a structured reviewer, a plain-language explanation, possible curriculum topics not covered in the file, your chosen number of fresh flashcards, and a challenging quiz. PDF, DOCX, PPTX, and TXT are supported. Processing capacity depends on the hosting service and AI provider.

Choose 1–50. Each regeneration replaces the current deck with new questions and answers.

✦

Learn from your own material

Lumi uses your extracted upload text to prepare every study aid together. It sends that text to the AI provider configured for LearnIT, then saves the text and bundle in My decks on this device.

AI study aids may contain mistakes. Do not upload patient information or other private data; check answers against your course resources.
