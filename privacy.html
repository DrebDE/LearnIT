Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026 · Last updated October 26, 2026

This policy describes what LearnIT handles when you use the site, why it is used, and which services may receive it. LearnIT is maintained by its site owner. Privacy questions or requests: 24ur0307@psu.edu.ph.

Read the Terms & ConditionsReturn to LearnIT

1. Information LearnIT handles

  • Account details: email address, account identifiers, and sign-in session information when you use email, Google, or Facebook sign-in.
  • Preferences and activity: selected study fields, theme, recent study history, and Lumi chat messages.
  • Study materials: files you select for extraction, extracted text, and the reviewers, flashcards, quizzes, and progress created from them.
  • Search details: the topic and selected field used for a literature search, plus the results returned by external indexes.
  • Technical information: basic request and device information may be processed by the hosting, authentication, content-delivery, and other connected providers that operate the site.

2. How information is used

LearnIT uses this information to sign you in, remember your preferences, show your local study history and decks, answer Lumi prompts, extract text from a file you selected, generate study aids, and search academic references. It is also used to maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

3. Browser storage and saved study data

LearnIT stores preferences, recent history, the Lumi conversation, and sign-in session information in your browser. Uploaded study materials and generated decks are saved in your browser’s local IndexedDB storage. This data is not synchronized to other devices by LearnIT. It remains in that browser until you delete it in LearnIT or clear that browser’s site data; uninstalling or changing browsers may also remove it.

Use “Clear chat,” the deck delete controls, and “Clear history” to remove the corresponding local records. Signing out does not automatically delete local decks or browser history.

4. Uploads and AI features

When you choose a PDF, DOCX, PPTX, or TXT file, its contents are sent to the LearnIT hosting server for text extraction. The app code does not save the original file or extracted study bundle in a LearnIT server database. Extracted text is returned to your browser, and when you ask Lumi to work with that content, the relevant text and prompt are sent through LearnIT’s server to the AI provider configured by the site owner.

The configured provider may process the request under its own privacy terms and retention settings. The provider and endpoint can be changed by the site owner. LearnIT cannot control the provider’s independent handling of requests. Do not upload patient information, personal records, or confidential content.

5. Lumi chat

Your message and recent conversation context are sent to the configured AI provider so Lumi can reply. Chat history is saved in local browser storage so it can appear when you return on that device. Use “Clear chat” to erase that local history. Avoid entering personal, patient, or confidential information in chat.

6. Academic search and external links

When you search in LearnIT, your search term and selected fields are sent to the LearnIT server. The server sends the search term, often combined with course-related terms, to academic indexes including NCBI (PubMed and Bookshelf), Europe PMC, and Crossref. LearnIT may keep search results temporarily in server memory to make repeat searches faster. If you click a source-search card or an external result, the query or page you open is sent to that third party under its own practices.

7. Service providers

LearnIT relies on Render for hosting; Supabase for account authentication; Google or Meta if you choose their sign-in option; the AI provider configured by the site owner for Lumi; and academic databases and content-delivery services to provide the site. Google Fonts and jsDelivr also deliver fonts and the browser library, and may receive technical connection data such as your IP address when a browser loads those assets. These providers may process technical information or requests as needed to deliver their services and under their own privacy terms. Some providers may process information in other countries.

8. Retention and deletion

Study materials, chat, preferences, and history stay in your browser until you delete them or clear its site data. Account records are handled by Supabase and the selected sign-in provider under their retention practices; contact LearnIT if you need help with an account request. Search caching is temporary and held in service memory. Hosting and providers may retain technical logs according to their own policies.

9. Your choices and privacy requests

You can choose whether to create an account, use guest access where available, provide a file to Lumi, or make an academic search. You can clear local history, chat, and saved decks using the controls described above. You may contact 24ur0307@psu.edu.ph to ask about access, correction, deletion, or another privacy request. Rights and procedures depend on applicable law and may have legal exceptions.

If the Philippine Data Privacy Act applies to you, the National Privacy Commission provides information about data-subject rights and complaint procedures at privacy.gov.ph/data-subject-rights.

10. Security and changes

LearnIT uses HTTPS on its hosted site and applies reasonable measures through its code and service providers. No website or storage system can guarantee absolute security. This policy may be updated as the service or its providers change; the latest update date will be shown at the top.
